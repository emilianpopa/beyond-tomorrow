The opportunity

Trust is the scarce asset in the AI & longevity era

The world is changing faster than anyone realises. Technology grows exponentially while people feel more dysregulated, disconnected and disinformed. Beyond Tomorrow is the fastest-growing new media company and community in longevity and AI, giving members the content, gatherings and tools to thrive. Attention plus trust is the moat, and it compounds.

The long-term play

Four moves, one flywheel

High-integrity content creates trust. Trust compounds into community, which becomes distribution and revenue. The summits connect capital to trillion-dollar opportunities, and we use that capital and distribution to build the tools. Two businesses, one de-risking the other.

Media
The trust engine

Podcast, documentary and newsletter. Original, high-quality, rigorous reporting.

Summits
The gathering place

High-growth community and major summits with a leading conference provider. Audience becomes movement.

Investment
The capital engine

5–10 SPVs a year, $1–5M each, into potential trillion-dollar deal-flow. BT sponsors and earns carry off the summits.

Tools
The endgame

Products for the community, once distribution and capital are proven.

Narrative

The story the numbers tell

How to read the model in plain terms. Written for you to walk an investor through, and clear enough to share.

Beyond Tomorrow is two businesses in one, and the model is built that way on purpose. A media and summit platform that already works and can stand on its own, and an investment engine on top that turns the audience and the summits into deal-flow and carry. The first de-risks the second.

The numbers that carry the whole story
  • $22.6 million of revenue by 2029. From $430k in 2026, and it is media that drives the growth.
  • Breakeven in 2027, profitable from 2028, reaching ~$9.2M EBITDA by 2029. A real, underwritable media business.
  • The SPV arm earns only a 0.8% fee here (~$320k by 2029). Its carry is real long-term upside, deliberately kept out of the headline.
  • $1.5M raise at $11M pre, $660k already committed. It takes the company to profit by 2028 and prepares the capital vehicle.
Read it as a base case plus upside

This is the most important point in the model, and it maps to the two kinds of investor in this round. The base case is media, summits and products, run against an audience Beyond Tomorrow already has, 185,000 listeners a month. The company reaches a real, profitable ~$22.6 million business by 2029, media-led. The SPV carry sits on top as upside, not in the numbers. So the investor who needs to see the numbers can underwrite the platform, and the investor who backs the founder buys the moonshot, and neither has to believe the other's case.

Where the upside is created: 2027 and 2028

2027 is the inflection. The Summit launches with a major conference provider, a satellite in June and the flagship in September, and the LP relationships for the capital vehicle get locked. 2028 is where the two engines both fire: the company reaches profitability, products launch, and the first SPVs are deployed. That is the year media compounds hardest, and it is what carries 2029 to $22.6 million.

Why the platform is credible

The media build is anchored to real, signed sponsors and a growing audience, and the 2028–29 media numbers are the ones Beyond Tomorrow has already modelled. The summit economics are grounded in the conference-provider partnership, which shares production cost. Products launch only once distribution is proven. The single biggest bet, the SPV carry, is named openly rather than buried, and it is the layer, not the foundation.

In one line: a media-led business reaching ~$22.6M by 2029, profitable from 2028, with the SPV carry as genuine long-term upside that is not even in the numbers. The base is underwritable, the moonshot is free.

Products Reasonable Aggressive 2029

  • Co-branded longevity products, from 2028 → $2.7M by 2029 (~50% COGS). No e-learning line.
  • Sign off: product timing, and whether in-house or licensed.

Investment / SPV Fees only

  • BT originates SPVs from summit deal-flow and earns a 0.8% management fee on AUM (Julian's input).
