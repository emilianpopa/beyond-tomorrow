The story the numbers tell

Beyond Tomorrow is a media-led business. Sponsorship, AdSense and the newsletter are the engine and do the heavy lifting; the summits and products grow alongside; and the investment arm earns management fees today while its real prize, the carry, sits as long-term upside that is deliberately not in these numbers.

The numbers that carry the story
  • $22.6M of revenue by 2029, from $430k in 2026, reconciled to the deck.
  • Media is the driver, growing to ~$11.6M by 2029. Summits add ~$8M, products ~$2.7M.
  • Breakeven in 2027, profitable from 2028, reaching ~$9.2M EBITDA by 2029.
  • The SPV arm earns only a 0.8% fee here (~$320k by 2029). Its carry is real long-term upside, kept out of the headline.
How to read it

The whole model is a business you can underwrite: a media company that scales to a multiple of eight figures, profitable, with real summits and a products line from 2028. The investment arm sits on top, earning fees on the capital it deploys; the carry on that capital is the moonshot, left as upside rather than baked into the forecast. So the "show me the numbers" investor gets a $22.6M media-led business, and the "I believe in you" investor gets the carry optionality for free.

2027 is the inflection: the summit launches with a major conference provider, and the LP relationships for the capital vehicle get locked. 2028 and 2029 are where media compounds hardest, the summits scale and the products land.

What is solid vs what to probe. The summit trajectory is grounded in a signed JV proposal. The media ramp to ~$11.6M and the cost build are bottom-up constructions flagged for sign-off. The SPV fee is Julian's 0.8%; carry is deliberately excluded.
